TOPIC vs LESSON — this distinction matters, get it right: a TOPIC is a major unit-level heading (e.g. a 'TOPIC 1'/'TOPIC 2' divider page, or a bold banner naming the unit) — there are usually only 2-5 per module. A LESSON is a smaller heading INSIDE a topic (e.g. 'LESSON 1', 'LESSON 2', 'LESSON 3'... or an 'INTRODUCTION LESSON') — a topic's own overview/TOC page will usually list its lessons (e.g. 'LESSON 1 ... 5', 'LESSON 2 ... 21', 'LESSON 3 ... 37'). A LESSON header is NEVER a new topic — put it in the CURRENT (most recently seen) topic's lessons array, keyed by its lesson number, even if that topic started in an earlier batch you don't have anymore (just re-emit the same topic number/title you'd use for it, and the caller will merge batches of the same topic together).
